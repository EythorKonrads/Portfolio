# Portfolio

Eythor Konradsson's portfolio site, plus a hidden game in which visitors
defend Earth from asteroids.

## Site

**Hero**:
The panel that introduces Eythor: profile photo, name, role and the
"Let's talk" link.
_Avoid_: Header, banner, intro

**Aurora**:
The northern-lights effect behind the whole page, shown only in the dark theme.
_Avoid_: Background animation, northern lights

## Game

**Asteroid**:
A rock heading for Earth, in one of three sizes: large, medium or small.
A destroyed large Asteroid breaks into medium ones, and a medium one into
small ones.
_Avoid_: Rock, meteor, enemy

**Armored Asteroid**:
A plated Asteroid that joins from Wave 20. Only the Ship's shots can break
it; Turret and Satellite shots glance off, so they don't aim at it. It
breaks into medium Asteroids.
_Avoid_: Metal rock, boss

**Population**:
How many people are alive on Earth: Earth's health. Asteroid hits reduce it,
and the game ends when it reaches zero: Earth explodes, then the end screen
shows.
_Avoid_: Health, HP, lives

**Ship**:
The player's craft, which flies freely around Earth and shoots Asteroids.
_Avoid_: Player, spaceship

**Wave**:
A round of Asteroids. Waves get harder, and each one ends with a visit to
the Shop.
_Avoid_: Level, round, stage

**Ore**:
What destroying an Asteroid earns, spent in the Shop. Larger Asteroids give
more.
_Avoid_: Credits, coins, money, points

**Score**:
The tally shown when the game ends. It is separate from Ore, which is spent.
_Avoid_: Points

**Scoreboard**:
The shared top 10 of all time, shown when the game ends. A Score that makes
it goes on with the player's initials: 2 or 3 letters, A–Z.
_Avoid_: Leaderboard, high score list, hall of fame

**Shop**:
Where Ore is spent on upgrades, between Waves.
_Avoid_: Store, upgrade menu

**Menu**:
Opened from the corner of the screen during a Wave or in the Shop; it
pauses the game. It offers Resume, the Scoreboard and End game.
_Avoid_: Pause screen, settings

**End game**:
Leaving a game from the Menu, straight back to the page with no Score shown.
_Avoid_: Close, quit, exit

### Starting a game

**Mini Earth**:
The small Earth orbiting the profile photo in the Hero; it starts the game.
_Avoid_: Moon, planet, launcher

**Takeover**:
Mini Earth growing until it covers the profile photo, while the photo or Mini
Earth is hovered or focused. It shrinks back if the pointer leaves.
_Avoid_: Hover animation, expand

**Launch**:
Earth gliding from the Hero to the centre of the screen as the game starts.
_Avoid_: Transition, zoom

### Upgrades

**Earth defences**:
Upgrades bought for Earth itself (Turrets, Satellites and later Sensors), as
opposed to Ship upgrades.
_Avoid_: Base, towers

**Turret**:
A gun on Earth that aims itself at Asteroids. Its fire rate and damage can
be upgraded.
_Avoid_: Tower, cannon

**Satellite**:
An armed craft orbiting Earth. Each one bought joins the Constellation. Its
fire rate and damage can be upgraded.
_Avoid_: Drone, moon

**Constellation**:
All the Satellites orbiting Earth together.
_Avoid_: Defence ring, swarm

**Sensor**:
An Earth defence that warns of incoming Asteroids earlier. Not in v1.
_Avoid_: Radar
