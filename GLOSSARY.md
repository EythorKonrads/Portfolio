# Portfolio

Eythor Konradsson's portfolio site, plus a hidden game in which visitors
defend Earth from asteroids.

## Site

**Hero**:
The panel that introduces Eythor: profile photo, name, role and the
"Let's talk" link.
_Avoid_: Header, banner, intro

**Aurora**:
The northern-lights effect behind the Hero, shown only in the dark theme.
_Avoid_: Background animation, northern lights

## Game

**Asteroid**:
A rock heading for Earth, in one of three sizes: large, medium or small.
A destroyed large Asteroid breaks into medium ones, and a medium one into
small ones.
_Avoid_: Rock, meteor, enemy

**Population**:
How many people are alive on Earth: Earth's health. Asteroid hits reduce it,
and the game ends when it reaches zero.
_Avoid_: Health, HP, lives

**Ship**:
The player's craft, which flies freely around Earth and shoots Asteroids.
_Avoid_: Player, spaceship

**Wave**:
A round of Asteroids. Waves get harder, and each one ends with a visit to
the Shop.
_Avoid_: Level, round, stage

**Ore**:
What destroying an Asteroid earns, spent in the Shop. Larger Asteroids give
more.
_Avoid_: Credits, coins, money, points

**Score**:
The tally shown when the game ends. It is separate from Ore, which is spent.
_Avoid_: Points

**Shop**:
Where Ore is spent on upgrades, between Waves.
_Avoid_: Store, upgrade menu

### Upgrades

**Earth defences**:
Upgrades bought for Earth itself (Turrets, Satellites and later Sensors), as
opposed to Ship upgrades.
_Avoid_: Base, towers

**Turret**:
A gun on Earth that aims itself at Asteroids. Its fire rate and damage can
be upgraded.
_Avoid_: Tower, cannon

**Satellite**:
An armed craft orbiting Earth. Each one bought joins the Constellation.
_Avoid_: Drone, moon

**Constellation**:
All the Satellites orbiting Earth together.
_Avoid_: Defence ring, swarm

**Sensor**:
An Earth defence that warns of incoming Asteroids earlier. Not in v1.
_Avoid_: Radar
